## LinkedIn Draft

This week I created two internal agents:

- a Resource Planning and Upskill Agent
- an OpsRabbit Pilot Manager Agent

Different workflows, same product lesson:

Useful internal AI agents need operating models, not just prompts.

For resource planning, the hard part is not "generate a bench report."

The hard part is understanding who is active, inactive, consultant, management, allocated, on bench, approaching bench risk, or eligible for a profile refresh and upskilling recommendation.

For pilot management, the hard part is not "summarize the meeting."

The hard part is tracking pilot stage, blocker, owner, next action, customer promise, technical readiness, and product learning.

That is where internal AI stops being a chat interface over messy data and starts becoming an operating system for the business.

My current view:

The serious question for an internal agent is not only what it can automate.

It is what operating model it preserves.
